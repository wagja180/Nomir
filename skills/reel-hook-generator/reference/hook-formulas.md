# 26 Reel Hook Formulas

Each formula is a pattern for the first 1-3 seconds (spoken line, on-screen text, or both) of a Reel. Pick formulas that fit the content, generate a few variations, then score them with the rubric below before picking a winner.

## Scoring rubric

Score every candidate hook 1-5 on each dimension, total out of 25.

| Dimension | 1 (weak) | 5 (strong) |
|---|---|---|
| Curiosity gap | Nothing left unanswered | Opens a question only the video answers |
| Specificity | Vague ("a few tips") | Concrete numbers, names, timeframes |
| Targeting | Speaks to anyone | Names the exact viewer/identity/pain |
| Pattern interrupt | Reads like every other caption | Breaks the scroll rhythm (visual, tone, or phrasing) |
| Stakes | Unclear why it matters | Clear cost of ignoring it / payoff for watching |

- **20-25**: ship as-is.
- **15-19**: rewrite the lowest-scoring dimension only, re-score.
- **below 15**: discard, generate a new formula instead of patching.

## The 26 formulas

### Callout & targeting
1. **Direct Callout** — "If you're a [identity], watch this." Names the viewer so they self-select.
2. **Negative Callout** — "Stop [common action] if you want [outcome]." Interrupts a habit mid-scroll.
3. **Relatable POV** — "POV: you just [relatable moment]." Borrows the POV format's built-in identification.
4. **Checklist Self-Audit** — "If you do any of these 3 things, stop." Invites viewers to count themselves in.

### Contrarian & pattern interrupt
5. **Contrarian Statement** — "[Popular belief] is wrong. Here's why." Takes a side against consensus.
6. **Unpopular Opinion** — "Unpopular opinion: [claim]." Signals conflict before the claim lands.
7. **Myth-Busting** — "[Myth] is a myth. Here's the truth." Promises correction of something assumed true.
8. **Trend-Jack Correction** — "Everyone's doing [trend] wrong. Here's the right way." Rides an existing trend's attention, then subverts it.

### Curiosity gaps
9. **Secret Reveal** — "Nobody is talking about [thing], and it's costing you [outcome]." Implies hidden information.
10. **Curiosity Open Loop** — "This one [change/habit] changed everything." Withholds the "what" until later.
11. **Flashforward Result** — Open on the finished result, voiceover: "Here's how I got here." Shows payoff before process.
12. **Cold Open / In Medias Res** — Start mid-action: "So this just happened..." Drops viewer into a scene already moving.
13. **Comparison / Versus** — "[Thing A] vs [Thing B]: nobody tells you this." Frames a choice the viewer has to resolve.

### Confession & authority
14. **Mistake Confession** — "I wasted [time/money] on [thing] before I learned this." Personal stakes build trust fast.
15. **"What I Wish I Knew"** — "What I wish I knew before [milestone]." Promises hindsight shortcuts.
16. **Authority/Credential** — "After [X years/reps], here's what actually works." Earns the claim before making it.
17. **Transformation Teaser** — "From [before state] to [after state] in [timeframe]." Compresses a story into a promise.

### Numbers & stats
18. **Numbered List Promise** — "3 things nobody tells you about [topic]." Sets a countable, finishable structure.
19. **Specific Stat** — "[X]% of people get this wrong." Borrows the authority of a number — must be real or clearly sourced.
20. **Time-Bound Promise** — "In the next 30 seconds, I'll show you [outcome]." Makes the watch-time cost explicit and small.

### Questions & challenges
21. **Direct Question** — "Do you ever [pain point]?" Forces an internal yes/no that keeps them watching for relief.
22. **Rhetorical Challenge** — "Bet you didn't know [fact]." Dares the viewer to be wrong.
23. **Warning Hook** — "Don't [action] until you watch this." Frames skipping the video as risky.

### Urgency & stakes
24. **FOMO/Urgency** — "This is changing — here's what to do before it does." Adds a ticking clock.
25. **Problem-Agitate** — "If you struggle with [pain], this is why." Diagnoses before prescribing.
26. **Pattern-Interrupt Visual Cue** — Line paired with an unexpected action/prop on screen: "Watch what happens when..." The hook is as much the visual as the words.

## Usage notes
- Generate hooks from at least 4-5 different formulas per request, not 26 — variety within a request matters less than fit.
- A formula is a skeleton, not a script to copy verbatim; always fill it with the real niche, number, or stake from the content at hand. Never ship a bracket unfilled.
- Formulas 9, 19, 24 require a true claim behind them — don't invent a stat or urgency that doesn't exist.
